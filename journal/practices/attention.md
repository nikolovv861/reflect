## opening
Where was your attention just before you opened this?
Did you put it there, or did it end up there?
What were you feeling just before it moved?

## addendum
This page is an attention inventory. The writer is tracking where their
attention went, whether they placed it deliberately or it was hijacked, and
what feeling came just before it moved.

Ask about the moment attention shifted: what preceded it, what it was pulling
them away from, or what the feeling underneath it was. Noticing is the entire
practice, so never ask them to fix it, resist it, or do better next time.
