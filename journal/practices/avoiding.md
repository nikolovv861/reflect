## opening
Name one thing you have been steering around.
You do not have to face it here. Just say what it is, and how you have
been keeping it at the edge of the day.

## addendum
This page is about something the writer has been avoiding -- a task, a
conversation, a feeling, a decision they keep walking past. They are naming
it, not solving it.

Ask about what sits underneath the avoidance: what the avoiding protects them
from, what it has quietly cost to keep it at arm's length, or what they
imagine would have to be true to turn toward it. Never tell them to stop
avoiding it, to face it, or to do better -- the naming is enough.
