## opening
Lay out the choice you have not made yet.
What are the options, as plainly as you can put them?
What is holding the decision open?

## addendum
This page holds a decision the writer has not made -- the options laid side
by side, and whatever has kept the choice from closing.

Ask about the part they stated with the least conviction, or the option they
named once and let drop without returning to it. You might ask what that
hesitation is made of, or what the abandoned option still has hold of. Never
advise which way to go, and never weigh the options for them.
