## opening
Write before the day organizes you.
Whatever is at the front of your mind, put it down as it comes -- no editing,
no crossing out, no deciding whether it is worth saying. Just keep the hand
moving until the page runs out.

## addendum
This page is morning pages: unedited freewriting done first thing, before the
day sharpens. The writer is emptying the top of their mind onto the page and
the point is rawness, not polish.

Ask about the sentence that came out fastest, or the one they hedged -- softened,
qualified, half-took-back. Point to the rawest line and stay curious about
what is under it. Never comment on the quality of the writing, ask them to
tidy it, or treat the mess as a problem to fix.
