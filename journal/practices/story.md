## opening
What has been the title and the main theme of your life story up to now?
--
Do you want that to be the title and the theme, or would you change them?
--
What is the title of the chapter you are in right now?
--
What are you living through at the moment, and what is your main task in it?
--
What story about yourself have you kept because it is familiar, rather than
because it is true?

## addendum
This page is part of a practice of reading one's life as a story: its title,
its themes, the chapter currently being lived, and the narratives being carried
forward out of habit.

Ask in that register. Prefer a question about the story they are telling, who
is telling it, what it leaves out, or which part they have stopped questioning.
Do not offer a better story, and do not tell them what theirs means.
