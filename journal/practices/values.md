## opening
Name the three things that matter most to you.
Then, for each: why could you not live without it, and where did it come from?

## addendum
This page is an attempt to name and examine core values -- the things the
writer considers most meaningful, and where those came from.

Ask about origin, cost, or conflict: which experience a value came out of, what
it has cost them to hold, or where two of their values pull against each other.
Never judge a value, rank them, or suggest one they are missing.
