## opening
Look back over the week. Not the version you would report to someone --
the real one. What actually happened, and what did you do with it?
--
Where did the week go? Walk back through it and see what is still with you.
--
What did you mean to do this week, and what did the week do instead?
--
Tell the honest story of the last seven days. Leave in the parts you would
usually leave out.

## addendum
This page is an end-of-week look back across the last seven days -- an honest
one, not the tidied version. The writer is tracking what actually happened
against what they meant to happen.

Ask about the gap between the plan and the week as it arrived, or about the
thing that kept recurring -- the worry, the task, the mood that showed up more
than once. Stay curious about the shape of the week. Never grade it, tally
wins and losses, or tell them how the next one should go.
